Universal wiring (works for Panasonic + all):
• IR LED (MUST for TV): Arduino D3 → 220Ω → 2N2222 Base | 5V → IR LED+ (940nm), IR LED− → 2N2222 Collector, Emitter → GND. Point LED at TV (5–10m). Short-range test: D3 → 100Ω → LED → GND works to ~3m.
• RF (optional): FS1000A VCC→5V GND→GND DATA→D12 + 17.3cm antenna.
